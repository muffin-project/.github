# Muffin

### A human-first personal agent built to make you more capable, not more dependent.

**One agent · your continuity · your rules**

</div>

Muffin is one owner-run personal agent that keeps the thread across time,
models, tools, and surfaces while authority stays explicit and under human
control.

## Community Preview

Muffin is currently in Community Preview and pre-DAY-1 hardening. The runtime
works; the product is not a general-release personal agent yet. Claims are
expected to come with reproducible evidence, and preview access does not imply
stability, maintainer authority, or product readiness.

## What the project is protecting

- **Continuity** — memory, unfinished work, and commitments survive restarts and
  model changes.
- **Provenance** — Muffin keeps track of who said what and what actually
  happened.
- **Authority** — understanding what a person may want never grants permission
  to act.
- **Portability** — models and surfaces can change without creating a different
  agent.
